# Staff Quick Start Guide

Posxpaybotx Restaurant POS

---

## Logging In

1. Open the POS on your tablet or terminal (your manager will give you the URL)
2. Enter your **4-digit PIN** on the login screen
3. Tap **Login**

Your PIN is assigned by your manager. If you forget it, ask a manager to reset it.

---

## The Main Screen

After login you will see a top navigation bar with these tabs:

| Tab | What It Does |
|-----|-------------|
| **New Order** | Create and ring up orders |
| **Tables** | View the floor plan and manage table assignments |
| **Kitchen** | View the kitchen display (KDS) |
| **Tickets** | Browse open, paid, and closed tickets |
| **Reports** | View sales reports (managers only) |
| **Orders** | Incoming online, QR, and scheduled orders |

The top-right corner shows: the current time, a dark mode toggle, a fullscreen button, and your name with a logout button.

## Taking an Order

### Step 1: Start a New Ticket

- Tap the **New Order** tab
- Select the order type: **Dine-In**, **Takeout**, **Delivery**, or **Bar Tab**
- For dine-in, select the **table number**

### Step 2: Add Items

- Tap a **category** on the left (Popular, Appetizers, Entrees, Sides, Drinks, Desserts)
- Tap an **item** to add it to the ticket
- To search for an item by name, use the **search bar** at the top of the menu

### Step 3: Add Modifiers

When you tap an item, a modifier panel may appear:

| Modifier Group | Examples |
|---------------|----------|
| Temperature | Rare, Medium Rare, Medium, Medium Well, Well Done |
| Protein | Add Chicken (+$4.99), Add Shrimp (+$6.99), Add Steak (+$7.99) |
| Sides | Fries, Coleslaw, Side Salad, Onion Rings (+$1.50) |
| Extras | Extra Cheese (+$1.50), Bacon (+$2.00), Avocado (+$2.50) |
| Sauce | Ranch, BBQ, Honey Mustard, Buffalo, Garlic Aioli (+$0.50) |
| Allergy/Special | Gluten Free, Dairy Free, Nut Free, No Onion |

Tap a modifier to add it. Tap **Done** when finished.

### Step 4: Special Instructions

Tap the **Notes** button at the bottom of the ticket to add any special instructions (e.g., "no ice", "birthday candle", "allergy: shellfish").

### Step 5: Send to Kitchen

Tap **Send to Kitchen** (or press **F8**). The order appears on the kitchen display.

## Changing an Order

### Edit Items

- Tap an item on the ticket to select it
- Tap **+** or **-** to change the quantity
- Tap the trash icon to remove it

### Apply a Discount

- Tap the **Discount** button at the bottom of the ticket
- Enter a percentage or dollar amount
- Tap **Apply**

### Hold an Order

If the customer isn't ready, tap **Hold** (or press **F11**). The order is saved and can be recalled later from the Tickets tab.

---

## Processing Payment

### Cash Payment

1. Tap **Pay Cash** (or press **F9**)
2. Enter the amount the customer gave you
3. The system calculates change automatically
4. The cash drawer opens
5. Give the customer their change and receipt

If cash discount mode is active, the cash price is **lower** than the card price. The discount is applied automatically.

### Card Payment

1. Tap **Pay Card** (or press **F10**)
2. The total is sent to the payment terminal
3. The customer inserts/taps/swipes their card on the terminal
4. Wait for approval — the ticket closes automatically
5. If tip adjustment is enabled, tips can be adjusted later

### Split Payment

1. Tap **Split** at the bottom of the ticket
2. Choose how to split: by amount, by seat, or evenly
3. Process each payment separately (cash or card)

### Partial Payment

1. Tap **Other** payment option
2. Enter the partial amount
3. The remaining balance stays open on the ticket

---

## Understanding Dual Pricing

When cash discount is active, you'll see **two prices** on screen:

- **Cash price** — the lower price (discount applied)
- **Card price** — the standard menu price

The correct price is applied automatically based on the payment method. You don't need to do anything extra.

The receipt prints a clear disclosure explaining the pricing.

## Table Management

### Viewing the Floor Plan

Tap the **Tables** tab to see the restaurant layout.

| Color | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| Green | Available |
| Red | Occupied |
| Blue | Reserved |
| Yellow | Needs cleaning |

### Assigning a Table

1. Tap an available (green) table
2. Enter the party size
3. Start the order — the table turns red (occupied)

### Transferring a Table

1. Tap the occupied table
2. Select **Transfer**
3. Tap the destination table

## Kitchen Display (KDS)

If you have kitchen display access:

### Viewing Orders

- Tap the **Kitchen** tab
- Orders appear as cards, newest on the left
- Filter by station: **All**, **Grill**, **Fry**, **Salad**, **Expo**

### Color Codes

| Color | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| White/Default | Just received |
| Yellow | Approaching time limit |
| Red | Overdue |

### Actions

- **Bump** — Mark an order as complete (it moves off the screen)
- **Fire Course** — Start preparation for a specific course
- **Pickup** — Notify front-of-house that the order is ready for pickup

## Time Clock

### Clocking In

1. Tap the menu icon (top-left) or use **Ctrl+T**
2. Select **Time Clock**
3. Tap **Clock In**
4. Your shift starts — the system records the time

### Clocking Out

1. Open **Time Clock**
2. Tap **Clock Out**
3. Your shift ends — the hours are recorded for payroll

### Viewing Your Hours

Your time clock records are visible in the Time Clock section. Managers can view all employee hours.

---

## Voiding and Refunds

### Voiding a Ticket (Manager Required)

If a ticket needs to be canceled before payment:

1. Open the ticket from the **Tickets** tab
2. Tap **Void**
3. Enter a reason
4. A manager may need to approve with their PIN

### Refund (Manager Required)

If a customer needs a refund after payment:

1. A manager processes refunds from the **Reports** tab or directly via the ticket
2. Server and cashier roles **cannot** process refunds directly

---

## Online & QR Orders

When an online or QR order comes in:

1. A notification appears on the **Orders** tab
2. Open the order to review it
3. Tap **Accept** to send it to the kitchen, or **Reject** if it can't be fulfilled
4. Once prepared, tap **Complete** to mark it ready for pickup or delivery

## Tips for Speed

- Use **number keys 1-6** to jump between menu categories
- Use **F8** to fire orders to the kitchen instantly
- Use **F9/F10** to go straight to cash or card payment
- Use **Ctrl+N** to start a new ticket without touching the screen
- Use the **search bar** to find items by name instead of scrolling

## Troubleshooting

### "Session expired" message
Your login expires after 12 hours. Log in again with your PIN.

### Terminal says "Declined"
Ask the customer for a different payment method. Do **not** run the same card multiple times — the system prevents duplicate charges automatically.

### Order not showing in kitchen
Make sure you tapped **Send to Kitchen** (F8). Check that the order status shows "Sent" on the ticket.

### Screen seems frozen
Tap **Esc** to close any open popups. If the screen is still unresponsive, refresh the browser page and log in again. Your open tickets are saved.

### Cash drawer won't open
Ask a manager. They can open the drawer manually from the admin panel or by using the hardware endpoint.

### Wrong item on a ticket
Tap the item on the ticket and tap the trash icon to remove it, then add the correct item. If the order has already been sent to the kitchen, notify the kitchen verbally and update the ticket.
